How to read this page

Each system answered the cases of the benchmarks it runs on. A grader compares an answer with the private gold data. It gives a score from 0 to 1, and a pass or a fail. The mean score is the average score: how close the answers were. The pass rate is the share of answers that were fully correct. A grader without partial credit shows the same number for both. In each chart, the bar is the mean score and the dot is the pass rate. Higher is better.

Glossary

  • rag-recall: Returned verbatim quotes cover the gold LegalBench-RAG character spans. The score is character recall.
  • rag-precision: Returned verbatim quotes are inside the gold LegalBench-RAG character spans. The score is character precision.
  • error rate: the share of runs that failed in the sandbox or in a grader. A failed run counts as a failed grade.
  • consistency: the share of repetitions that gave the same answer for a case, averaged over the cases. n/a with one repetition.
  • latency s: the average time of one run, in seconds, sandbox start included.
  • model calls per run: the average number of model calls in one run, safety-model calls included.
  • harness cost, judge cost per run: the average cost of one run, and of grading it, as the provider reported. n/a for a free local model.
  • total cost, all runs: (harness cost + judge cost per run) × the number of runs: the bill for this system in this benchmark.
