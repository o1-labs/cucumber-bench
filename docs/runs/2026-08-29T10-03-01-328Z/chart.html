How to read this page

Every system answered the cases of the suites it lists; the comparison table of a suite names the systems that ran it. A grader compares each answer with private gold data and gives a score from 0 to 1 and a pass (yes or no). The mean score says how close the answers came on average; the pass rate says how often an answer was fully correct. A grader with no partial credit has the same value for both. Tune by the mean score; claim by the pass rate. In each chart the bar is the mean score and the dot is the pass rate.

  • direct — One model call. The raw input goes to the model. There is no safety stage. This is the baseline. Models used: qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b.
  • cite-v1 — The citation harness. Step 4: the same answer as direct, then a greedy check of every sentence: the citations become the smallest set of passages that supports it, and a sentence no passage supports is dropped. Models used: qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b.

Glossary

  • str-em — Every gold short answer appears in the output. The score is the share of sub-questions answered.
  • citation-recall — Every sentence is supported by the passages it cites. The score is the share of supported sentences.
  • citation-precision — Every citation is necessary: its passage supports the sentence and is not redundant. The score is the share of such citations.
  • consistency — the share of repetitions that gave the same answer for a case, averaged over cases. — with one repetition.
  • latency s — average wall time of one run, in seconds, including the sandbox start.
  • model calls per run — average model calls a run made, safety-model calls included.
  • harness cost, judge cost per run — average cost of one run and of grading it, as the provider reported it. — for a free local model.
  • total cost, all runs — (harness + judge cost per run) × the number of runs: the bill for this system in this benchmark.
